**Precedence (per AGENTS.md):** G21 / M22 override M18 wherever they differ.

**Page citation convention:** M22 and M18 are cited by *printed* page number
(PDF page = printed page + 2 for M22, + 4 for M18). G21 has no printed numbers,
so it is cited by PDF slide number.

**Dispositions used:** `TEACH CURRENT + UPDATE NOTE` (teach current value; render a
"What changed since 2018" card) · `2018-ONLY, DROP` · `UNCHANGED` ·
`CURRENT-ONLY, TEACH` (new content with no 2018 counterpart) · `SME REVIEW`.

## 2. Summary of "What changed since 2018" cards the app should render

1. **Recovery position removed from the CPR/AED flow** (item 19) — post-ROSC: supine, monitor, AED stays on.
2. **Lone rescuer never leaves the casualty to fetch an AED** (item 3) — 2018 allowed "get it if visible and nearby".
3. **Chain of Survival grew from five to seven rings** (item 22) — Prevention and Recovery added.
4. **Speaker-phone 995 call + dispatcher-assisted CPR + myResponder** (items 2, 38).
5. **Child defibrillation: paediatric dose attenuation now for ages 1–12** (was 1–8), pad separation now "at least 2 cm" (was 1–2 cm), anterior-posterior placement whenever only adult pads are available (items 31–32).
6. **AED re-analysis every 2 minutes made explicit** (item 21).
7. **Updated national statistics (2019 registry)** (item 39).

Everything else that the 2018 manual teaches about compressions (site, 4–6 cm, 100–120/min,
full recoil, ≤ 10 s rests after ~100 compressions), gasping recognition, AED hazards, chest
preparation, adult pad placement, shock flow and handover is **unchanged** and may be taught
with dual citations.

## 3. Requires qualified SRFAC instructor / clinical SME approval

These items are ambiguous, contradictory between current sources, or beyond what I can
verify from the documents supplied. They must NOT be presented to learners as settled
instruction until an SME rules on them. (Rule applied: never invent medical instruction.)

| # | Item | Why it needs SME sign-off |
|---|------|---------------------------|
| S1 | **Child pad-selection rule: age vs weight** | G21 slide 23 says child *under 25 kg* → child pads if available; M22 pp. 44–45 says paediatric dose-attenuation for *ages 1–12*; M18 p. 34 said *1–8*. The app needs one learner-facing rule (age, weight, or both) — SME must decide the canonical formulation. |
| S2 | **Infant (< 1 yr) defibrillation when only adult pads exist** | G21 slide 23: manual defibrillator preferred, else *child* pads. | S3 | **Child/infant HCP rule "pulse < 60/min → treat as arrest"** | Appears only in the G21 summary table (slide 23) with no elaboration in either manual. Confirm whether it belongs in the app's HCP-labelled child/infant awareness module. |
