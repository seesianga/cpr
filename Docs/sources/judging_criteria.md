Hi spatial hackers, some more details for how your apps will be judged tomorrow!

Your judges will come from a range of backgrounds, including academia and industry, and with expertise spanning design, engineering, and business. Please be prepared to elevator pitch and show your app succinctly to each of the judges, in **three minutes or less**. Your spatial apps will be judged by six categories, each of which will be awarded a first, second, and third prize based on the evaluations of the judging panel: 

🎨  **Best interface design:**

In this category, apps will be judged on how intuitive and seamless the experience is to users.

💻 **Best technical implementation:**

In this category, apps will be judged on how well they technically implement spatial computing features.

📖 **Best storytelling:**

In this category, apps will be judged on how well they communicate a narrative around their application. 
🎯 **Best problem-solution fit:**

In this category, apps will be judged on how well they align the spatial computing application to the intended use case to solve a real-world problem.

💡**Most novel:**

In this category, apps will be judged on how differentiated they are from previous spatial computing applications.

🎈**Most fun:**

In this category, apps will be judged on how much they delight their users.
